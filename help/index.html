Help

Short answers to the usual questions.

How do I add a drink?

Tap the red + button. Snap the drink in front of you, or pick a photo you already have. Coaster cuts the drink out and asks what it is and where you had it. Rate it if you like, add a line, and it goes on your shelf.

To start with older photos, use “Find my drinks” and Coaster looks through your library for drink photos. You approve each one before it is added.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Finding drinks in your photos and cutting them out happens on your iPhone. Your original photos are never uploaded. To work out the city, the location saved in a photo is looked up with Apple’s map service, and only the city and country are kept. The privacy policy has the detail.

How do friends work?

Each person has a short code, like K7Q4-2MZD. Send yours from the Friends tab, or type a friend’s code to add them. Friends need iCloud to be on.

Who sees my drinks?

Nobody, until you turn sharing on. After that, anyone who has added your code sees the drinks you share: the drink, the bar, the city, the day, your rating and your note. They never see the time, the photo’s location or the original photo.

Each drink has its own setting: friends, close friends, or only me. Changing a drink to only me removes it from your friends’ feeds.

How do I cancel the free trial?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Coaster, and tap Cancel. Do it at least 24 hours before the 7 days are up and you won’t be charged. If you said yes to a reminder, Coaster sends one before the trial ends. Your shelf stays free either way.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Profile › Export my data gives you every drink as a spreadsheet file.

How do I delete my data?

Profile › Delete everything removes your drinks, friends and settings from your phone and takes your shared drinks off iCloud. Deleting the app also removes everything on your phone. To remove your friend code or comments you left for friends, ask us.
