A shelf of the drinks you loved

Every drink becomes a cut-out sticker standing on its own coaster. It keeps the bar, the city and the day, plus your rating and a line about it, so “that red thing in Barcelona” is one search away.

  • Snap the drink in front of you, or start with photos you already have.
  • Rate it the way you would to a friend: best I’ve had, great, fine, not again.
  • No counts of how much, no streaks, no clock times.

Trips, and a passport for them

Coaster spots when a run of drinks happened somewhere else and offers to keep them together as a trip. Each country you drink in gets a stamp in your passport.

With Coaster Pro your shelf becomes a poster, ready to print.

Friends, if you want them

Add a friend with a short code and see the drinks they choose to share: one drink, one line, one place. Raise a glass or leave a comment.

  • Sharing is off until you turn it on, and any drink can stay just yours.
  • Friends see the day, never the time.
  • No follower counts and no public profile.
