Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

The short version

  • Your shelf lives on your iPhone. Coaster has no account to create and no servers of its own.
  • Photos are scanned and cut out on your phone. Original photos are never uploaded.
  • If you share a drink with friends, a small copy of it goes through Apple’s iCloud: the drink, the bar, the city, the day and a small picture. Never the time, never the exact location, never the original photo.
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your card.
  • No ads, no advertising trackers, and we don’t sell anything about you.

What stays on your phone

Everything you keep in Coaster is stored on your iPhone: the drink stickers, the drink names, ratings, notes, bars, cities, trips, prices you type and the day you had each drink. It is included in your iPhone’s own backups, such as iCloud Backup if you use it, like any other app’s data. Coaster does not copy it to anyone else.

Camera and photos

Coaster asks for the camera to snap the drink in front of you, and for your photo library so it can find drinks in photos you already have.

  • Finding drinks, reading labels and menus, and cutting the drink out of the photo all happen on your phone, using Apple’s built-in tools. Your photos are not uploaded to us or to anyone.
  • Only the drinks you approve are added. For a photo from your library, Coaster keeps the sticker and a small thumbnail and remembers which photo it came from. For a photo you take in Coaster, it may also keep the original on your phone so the drink can be cut out again.
  • To name the city, Coaster reads the date and the location saved in the photo. It sends that location to Apple’s map service, which answers with the city and country. Coaster keeps the city and country and throws the location away. Photos with no location fall back to your phone’s region.
  • If you save a sticker or card to your photos, Coaster asks to add to your library. It doesn’t read anything when it does this.

Coaster does not ask for your location, and it does not use your contacts.

Friends

Friends are optional and work through Apple’s iCloud, so you need to be signed in to iCloud on your iPhone. Sharing is off until you turn it on, and you can keep any drink to yourself from its details.

When you use friends, these are stored in Coaster’s area of Apple’s iCloud:

WhatWhat it holds
Your profileYour friend code, the name you show friends, and the random ID iCloud gives you for Coaster. It is not your Apple ID, email or phone number.
Each drink you shareThe drink name, what the bar called it, the bar, city and country, your rating, your one-line note, the day and month (midnight, no time), who it is shared with, and a small 256 pixel picture of the sticker.
Comments and cheersThe text of a comment (up to 280 characters), which drink it is on, your friend name and when you posted it. A cheer is just your friend name on a drink.

Never shared: the original photo, the time of day, the photo’s location, your trip names, prices, or any drink you keep to yourself.

Who sees it. Anyone who has your code can add you and see the drinks you share with friends. A drink marked for close friends is shown only to the people on that list. Give your code to people you’d show your shelf to. If you stop sharing a drink, it is removed from iCloud.

Subscriptions

Coaster Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, card or Apple ID. Coaster uses Superwall, a subscription service, to show the subscription screen and to know whether you are subscribed. Superwall receives a random ID for the app, basic device and app details (model, iOS version, language, region, app version), which screens were shown, and what was bought or restored. It does not receive your photos, drinks, bars or notes. See Superwall’s privacy policy.

“Ask about this drink”

Questions about a drink are answered on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence, where your phone supports it. Nothing is sent to us or to another AI service. Before we ever change that, we will update this policy and say exactly what is sent.

Reminders

If you start a free trial and say yes to a reminder, Coaster schedules one notification on your phone before the trial ends. It is set on your phone; no notification service of ours is involved.

Analytics and ads

Coaster has no ads and no advertising or analytics SDKs. It doesn’t track you across apps or websites. If you have chosen to share analytics with developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may pass us crash reports and usage figures that Apple has made anonymous.

Export and delete

  • Export: Profile › Export my data gives you every drink as a spreadsheet file (CSV).
  • Delete: Profile › Delete everything removes your drinks, stickers, friends and settings from your phone, and removes every drink you shared from iCloud.
  • Your friend code profile and any comments or cheers you left on friends’ drinks are not removed by that button yet. Ask us and we will remove them.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything stored on your phone.

Age

Coaster is about drinks and is meant for people of legal drinking age where they live. It is not meant for children.

Changes

If what Coaster collects changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the app changes.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub. A contact email is coming.
