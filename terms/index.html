These terms cover your use of the Coaster app for iPhone. By using Coaster you agree to them, and to Apple’s standard licence for App Store apps, which also applies.

Who can use Coaster

Coaster is about drinks. You need to be of legal drinking age where you live to use it. Please drink responsibly; Coaster keeps a record of drinks you enjoyed and never encourages you to drink more.

Free and Pro

Keeping your shelf is free. Coaster Pro is an optional subscription that adds trip and year recaps, the poster and cards without the wordmark. What Pro includes is shown in the app before you buy.

Subscriptions and the free trial

  • Coaster Pro is sold through Apple as an auto-renewing subscription, yearly or monthly. The price for your country is shown in the app before you confirm.
  • Both plans start with a 7-day free trial. If you don’t cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, your Apple ID is charged and the subscription starts.
  • The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the period ends. Your Apple ID is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Manage or cancel at any time in your iPhone’s Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Payments and refunds are handled by Apple under its terms. To ask for a refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your content

Your photos, drinks and notes are yours. Coaster stores them on your phone. When you share a drink with friends, you give Coaster permission to store and show that drink to the friends you chose, as explained in the privacy policy.

Please share only photos and words you have the right to share, and be kind in comments. Don’t use Coaster to harass anyone or to post anything illegal.

The app as it is

We work to keep Coaster running well, but it is provided as it is, without promises that it will always be available or free of mistakes. Drink facts and answers in the app are for fun and may be wrong. Keep your own copy of anything you can’t lose; Profile › Export my data gives you a spreadsheet of your drinks.
